What is Onboardie?

A quick introduction before you dive in.

Onboardie is an AI onboarding coach built to help new hires get up to speed faster and to save managers time. For new hires, it answers day-to-day questions through a conversational AI coach, tracks their first 90 days, and lays out a clear learning plan.

For managers, it replaces repeating the same explanations one-on-one. Upload your existing onboarding notes, and with a single click, AI structures them into a clean, organized knowledge base ready for your review. Once you publish it, new hires get instant, consistent answers instead of waiting on you, and you can see exactly what they are asking about most, so onboarding knowledge stops living in scattered documents and starts working for you.

Why we are testing with you: you are trying an early prototype, and this round of testing is how we gather honest feedback, catch what is confusing or not working as expected, and understand onboarding challenges at companies like yours. Anything you notice, large or small, helps shape what we build next.

Before you start

Two things to have ready before you sign up.

Which email should I use? Whatever you are comfortable with, such as Gmail, Outlook, or anything else. It does not need to be your work email.
Have your start date ready: if you are testing as a New Hire, you will be asked for one. Any date works; it is simply what your dashboard uses to count your onboarding days.
Managers, we have included a mock-up role document for you to test the Upload Role Content feature. You will find the download link once you reach that section below.

Getting started

The same four steps for everyone, and you will land on your own dashboard.

  1. Go to the sign-up page

    Enter your test email address and choose a password.

  2. Choose your role

    Pick New Hire or Manager. This decides which portal you will see.

  3. New Hires: add your start date

    This sets your day counter to day zero. Managers skip this step.

  4. You are all set

    New Hires land on their Onboarding Dashboard. Managers land on their Manager Dashboard.

Confirmation emails currently come from a shared address rather than a custom one, so they can sometimes land in spam or take a few minutes to arrive. If nothing shows up right away, check spam before trying again.

What you will see

Switch between the two views below to see what each role experiences.

Your Onboarding Dashboard

Your home base. A day counter tracks your first 90 days on the job, but it only counts actual working days, so Saturdays, Sundays, and public holidays in Bavaria are skipped and do not move the counter forward. This keeps it accurate to your real time in the role, rather than the calendar date. Next to it sits a calendar, your recent progress, your next steps, and quick links to everything else.

Chat with your AI Coach

Ask it anything about your role. It only answers using content your manager has approved, and every reply carries a "Verified by your manager" badge. If it does not know something, it says so clearly and points you to your manager instead of guessing.

Try asking: "What should I focus on in my first week?"

Learning Plan

A 90-day checklist, broken into Week 1, Week 2, Month 1, Month 2, and Month 3. Check off tasks as you go. Your progress is saved across logins.

Try checking off a task, then log out and back in to confirm it was saved.

Resources and Tools

A searchable list of every tool you will need, grouped by what each one does, plus expert contacts and links. Duplicate tools are merged so you are not scrolling past the same thing twice.

Who to Contact

A directory of your colleagues, grouped by department, with email and phone shown where available. Missing details show a placeholder so you know it is on its way, not broken.

Role Overview & Q&A

A summary of your role, three highlight cards (Core Function, Your Impact, What Success Looks Like), and FAQs grouped by topic (Scheduling, Tooling, Role-related) so you can find answers without scrolling through everything.

Settings

Edit your name, role title, or start date whenever you need to.

Manager Dashboard

Your published roles at a glance (kept clearly separate from example roles), a progress table for your new hires, and a "Most common questions this month" chart. Click any chart segment to see the real, anonymous questions behind it.

Upload Role Content

Paste your onboarding notes directly, or upload a PDF, Word, TXT, or RTF file. Click "Structure This" and AI organizes it into five sections: Role Overview, Learning Plan, FAQs, Tools & How to Use Them, and Who to Contact. You name the role yourself, so nothing overwrites an existing one by accident.

Try it with our mock-up role: download the HR Specialist PDF, then upload it here and see how it gets structured.

Review & Approve

The structured content shows up read-only. Click the pencil icon on any section to edit it inline. When it looks right, click "Approve & Publish" to make it visible to your new hires, or start from a copied example role instead of writing from scratch.

Manage Content

Every published role, with when it was last updated and a one-click edit button. Example roles are listed separately as view-only references.

Try publishing a role, then go back and edit it to see the update reflected.

Worth knowing before you test

This is an early prototype, and here is where the rough edges currently are.

  • Confirmation emails can be delayed. They are sent from a shared address for now, not a custom one, so check spam if nothing arrives within a few minutes.
  • The new-hire progress table shows sample data. On the Manager Dashboard, that table is currently seeded with demo hires rather than your real ones. Live data is coming in a future update.
  • Your content stays private to your workspace. Whatever you upload or publish is visible only to people using the same email domain as you.
